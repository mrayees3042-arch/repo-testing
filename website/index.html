01 / CONNECTION

Set the connection

Enter the PLC IP address, rack, slot (including slot 0 for modern controllers), and port 102. Run network ping and S7 diagnostic handshakes before establishing persistent live communication.

02 / ORGANIZATION

Organize your tags

Structure memory addresses (DB, Marker, Input, Output) into clean, named tag tables. Fully canonical preview verifies memory addresses (e.g. DB1.DBX0.0) without collision.

03 / DIAGNOSTICS

Check status & diagnostics

Review live device state (RUN/STOP/UNKNOWN), communication roundtrip latency in milliseconds, and detailed event logging with automatic connection recovery.

02 / LIVE GROUPS

Group your tags.
Watch them live.

Build a view around your equipment or the task at hand. Multi-tag cross-table grouping lets you monitor related signals in real-time cards with dynamic sparkline trends.

01 Make it your own

Select tags across different tables, name your group (e.g. Line 01 essentials), and customize membership dynamically.

04 / OEE & TEEP · A WORKED EXAMPLE

Measure performance.
See capacity.

Standardize the math that drives operational decision making. Understand how effectively planned time is used versus your full calendar potential.

OEE Overall Equipment Effectiveness
Availability × Performance × Quality

Measures how effectively scheduled and planned production time is utilized during operational hours.

TEEP Total Effective Equipment Performance
OEE × Utilization

Measures true bottom-line effectiveness across all 24 hours of total calendar time (including unscheduled hours).

1 / VERIFIED INPUTS
Availability 90% · Performance 95% · Quality 98% · Scheduled 16 of 24 hours
Utilization = 16 ÷ 24 = 66.67%. The exact same underlying machine performance is benchmarked against total calendar capacity.
2 / EFFECTIVENESS DURING PLANNED PRODUCTION
83.79%
OEE = 0.90 × 0.95 × 0.98 = 83.79%
3 / EFFECTIVENESS ACROSS TOTAL CALENDAR TIME
55.86%
TEEP = 83.79% × (16 ÷ 24) = 55.86%
